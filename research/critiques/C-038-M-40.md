"legacy neighbor means practically never prune" while the oracle says green. That is the same class

- Reuse the exact-boundary shape from `rt_z_5`: after capturing `real_last_seq`, assert the legacy-neighbor candidate is prunable at `covered=real_last_seq` and not prunable at `covered=real_last_seq - 1` (guard `real_last_seq > 0`).
