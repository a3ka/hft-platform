## Verified claims

1. `crates/ops/src/watchdog.rs::check_serving_silence` computes signed
   deltas and raises `ServingSilence` at `Critical` exactly when
   `Δattempts > 0 ∧ Δrefusals_supported > 0 ∧ Δsuccesses == 0`.  In
   `watchdog_cycle.rs`, every `Present` sample replaces
   `prev_serving_heartbeat`, so a negative counter delta is a non-alerting
   restart observation and the next sample is compared with the new anchor.
2. `check_serving_heartbeat_missing` distinguishes `ConfiguredMissing`, and
   `check_serving_heartbeat_stale` uses
   `serving_heartbeat_warn_ms`/`serving_heartbeat_crit_ms` with Warning then
   Critical escalation.  The heartbeat writer emits `schema: 1`, the four
   named counters, and runs at the configured compose period (10 seconds by
   default).
3. The per-instance producer claim is covered on both ingress routes.
   `red_m89_counters_instance` executes the v1 serving path; the legacy suite
   proves `l1`--`l3` normally and `l4` with `--features testing`.  All four
   passed.  `red_m89_heartbeat_entrypoint` executes the binary and confirms
   the compose path, atomic heartbeat, schema, and counter growth.
4. `OPS-I-10` remains the applicable live FA invariant: the heartbeat oracle
   observes sample values after the real serving path, not merely its declared
   fields.
5. No §7.1 row is required for `OPS-I-11`. `watchdog.rs` deliberately places
   `WD-*` incidents outside `alerts::ALERT_RULES`, while §7.1 is the
   P0/P1/P2 Prometheus/Alertmanager parity canon. Adding a row without a
   corresponding metric rule would falsely claim that this separate watchdog
   alert participates in that parity.
6. The stated limitation is accurate: the Dockerfile builds/copies recorder,
   journal-retention, gateway-serve, gateway-checkpoint, and wsprobe, but not
   `ops-watchdog`; `deploy.yml` neither builds nor launches that binary.
   The cron wrapper therefore reports a missing binary instead of computing
   the serving alerts, as TD-231 states.

## Advisory

The branch truthfully records that the M-91 milestone and several source/test
comments still call the serving condition `OPS-I-8`. They do not alter the
runtime rule, but a future owner of those comments should retag them to
`OPS-I-11`; otherwise later readers can recreate the just-corrected category
confusion. This is advisory because the change accurately documents the
current mismatch and its executable behavior is unchanged.
