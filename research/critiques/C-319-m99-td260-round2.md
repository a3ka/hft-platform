## C-318 B-1: closed by execution

`M-99` §4(12)(d), §7, §8, §9, §10 task 2d, §11 and §14 agree on one behavior:
before any fetch, `RESTORE` must observably exist and be empty. Otherwise the wrapper
writes `ok=0`, empty `digest`, `reason=transport-failed`, `checked=0`, exits 5,
emits stderr alert, and never calls the reader.

`SR` gives the directory a complete previous restore and makes `cp` report success
without copying current-run bytes. Its setup guard proves both preconditions that
matter: the prior restore is complete and the fake `rm`/`cp` are first in the local
`PATH`. `SR-1` makes `rm` fail; `SR-2` makes it return success without removing
anything. `SR-M` rejects the seven weakened outcomes, including success, reader call,
missing state, non-empty digest, wrong reason/code, and `checked=3`.

## Class check, not merely the new cell

The remaining admissible ways to credit a selected index without its current delivery
are covered by distinct executable predicates:

- `PS` pins membership on a 108419-byte listing beyond the pipe buffer and checks each
  selected index/form; `DV` makes a selected fetch return 0 without materialising its
  bytes, for both a whole index and one form of a dual-form index.
- `SR` closes the historical-byte path before selection; lock acquisition precedes
  cleanup, so a legitimate second wrapper cannot cross the empty-directory check.
- Existing `A10` protects a missing selected form; `A12`/`A13` protect missing or
  altered sidecars; `A1` protects references to the cold source rather than delivered
  bytes. These are not silently reclassified as new TD-260 coverage.

The model does not claim to defend an unrelated privileged process that writes into
`RESTORE` after the empty-directory observation. That is outside the single-wrapper,
lock-held transport model, not a current-run fetch path; it is not represented as a
closed safety property. The named §11 limits remain honest: local probing does not
prove the network, sampling does not test unselected segments, `PF` remains
probabilistic rather than RED, `SR` abstracts the cause of cleanup failure, and no
general rsync timeout is claimed.
